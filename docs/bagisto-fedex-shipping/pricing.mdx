## What's Included

Every license includes:

- **Lifetime access** — every version we ever release is yours to keep, forever
- **All features** — no feature gating, no "pro" tiers, no upsells
- **All 11 FedEx service types** — Ground, Home Delivery, 2Day, Overnight, International and more

- **Unlimited products & orders** — no per-order, per-label, or per-rate limits from us
- **Unlimited users** — everyone on your team can use the package

You only pay FedEx for the labels you actually print. We never markup shipping costs.

## License Terms

### Project-Based Licensing

- **Solo** — 1 project. A single store — however many domains, servers, or environments you need to run it.
- **Agency** — Unlimited projects

Local development, staging, and CI environments are **unlimited** and do not count toward your project limit.

### Lifetime, No Renewals

This is the part that matters:

- **You buy once, you own it forever.** Every version we release — today and in the future — is yours to download and use indefinitely. No subscription trap. No renewal emails. No "your license expired" popups in production.
- **All future updates included** — every feature, fix, and new FedEx API capability we ship
- **Email support** — questions, bug reports, and feature requests
- **Priority support** — Agency licenses get faster response times

There is nothing to renew. Your code never stops working, and you never pay us again.

### Source Code

Bagisto FedEx Shipping is distributed as compiled PHP source. You have full access to read and modify the code for your own use within licensed projects. Redistribution, resale, or use in unlicensed projects is prohibited.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What counts as a "project"?

A project is a single store or product. If you build `store-a.com` and `store-b.com` as two separate Bagisto installations, that's two projects. A single Bagisto multichannel setup serving one brand is one project — however many domains or servers it runs on.

### Can I upgrade from Solo to Agency?

Yes. Contact us at [hello@ashraficlabs.com](mailto:hello@ashraficlabs.com) and we'll credit your existing license toward an Agency plan.

### Do I need a FedEx shipping account?

Not to get started. Free test keys from the [FedEx Developer Portal](https://developer.fedex.com) work immediately against the sandbox — no FedEx account or credit card needed. A FedEx shipping account is only required to move to production (real prices, real labels).

### Do I pay for FedEx API usage?

No. FedEx's rate, track, and address validation APIs are free to use. Labels are billed by FedEx at your negotiated rates when you print them — we never add anything on top.

### What does "lifetime" actually mean?

Exactly what it says. Pay once and every version of the package — including all future releases — is yours forever. There is no update window that expires and no renewal to remember.

### Why not a subscription?

Because we don't believe in holding your code hostage. You paid for the tool — you should be able to use it forever. Selling lifetime licenses keeps things simple for you and keeps us honest about earning your purchases with real improvements.
